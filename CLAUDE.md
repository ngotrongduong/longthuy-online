# Long Thuy online income — guide for Claude

On the owner's computer this repository is the folder `C:\Ai kiem tien`, where everything of the business is kept.
`NEXT_STEPS.md` is the work queue. `PLAN.md` is the plan: read it first, and keep it current (the phase, the KPI
row of the week, `LEDGER.md` each month). Claude decides and does the work. The owner only does what needs his identity.

- **Language.** Replies to the owner are in Vietnamese. Files, listings and messages to clients are in English
  (unless the client writes in another language).
- **Claude decides the business end to end (the owner's order, 2026-10-26)**: jobs, prices, proposals, replies,
  listings, delivery, as long as it makes a profit over what the business spends. Weekly self-review in
  `LESSONS.md`. Spending money stays the owner's call: Claude puts the numbers to him in one line.
- **Claude presses the buttons (the owner's order, 2026-10-26).** On the platforms Claude clicks Publish, Save and
  Continue and makes the choices itself, then reports. It does not send the owner to press them.
- **What only the owner does.** Anything about money (payment methods, payouts, bank or PayPal, purchases), and
  what Claude's rules forbid it: signing in or entering passwords, bot checks ("Press & Hold"), identity documents,
  government or tax ID numbers. For a legal declaration or terms box, Claude asks a one-line yes in chat, then ticks
  it itself.
- **Client work.**
  - Each order goes in `orders/<platform>-<id>/`, which git ignores: the brief, the client's files, the work and the
    delivery.
  - Roblox work is tested in Roblox Studio on the owner's machine before delivery.
  - Every delivery has a README (how to install it, what it does, what it does not do).
- **Quotes.** Fixed scope, fixed price, a delivery date we can keep. When the scope is unclear, quote the smallest
  package and say what is left out.
- **The weekly round** is in `PLAN.md`. Each week changes ONE thing in the listings and writes why in the KPI row.
