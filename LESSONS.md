Each Monday: the numbers, profit against spending, what worked, what did not and why, the lessons, and what changes
next week. Keep each week short. Lessons that still hold go in "Standing lessons" at the bottom.

## Week of 2026-10-26 (setup)

- **Numbers**: Fiverr G1 and G2 live (0 impressions yet). Upwork profile public, Agency created, 2 proposals sent,
  121 Connects left. Gross 0. Spending this business: Upwork Freelancer Plus USD 9.99.
- **What worked**: listings filled straight from `listings/`; covers uploaded by fetching the public PNG in page
  script; refs and `getBoundingClientRect` beat screenshots for clicking.
- **What did not**: Fiverr shows a "Press & Hold" bot check when pages are opened fast; Fiverr titles reject commas
  and hyphens; Upwork Roblox jobs are few (mostly YouTube animation); a proposal costs 11–19 Connects, so 2–3 a day
  is not affordable on 100 Connects a month.
- **Lessons**: open pages slowly; read the whole post and the client before spending Connects; skip AI-banned
  posts, hires already made, and mass posters paying a few dollars an hour; at most 2 strong-fit proposals a day.
- **Next week**: daily proposals by the scheduled task; a portfolio item (a Roblox system clip and a rigged model)
  so 3D clients can see our work; watch the first Fiverr impressions before changing a listing.
