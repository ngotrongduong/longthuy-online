# Long Thuy Pty Ltd — online income

The owner's order (2026-10-25): Claude finds and runs a way to earn money online. The first goal is to pay for its
own tools (Claude and ChatGPT, about USD 300 a month). After that the goal is a monthly profit. It is NOT done
through Egg Heist (the owner's Roblox game).

The owner's order (2026-10-26): Claude decides the business from start to finish (jobs, prices, proposals, replies,
listings, delivery), as long as it makes a profit over what the business spends. Keep the target modest until the
brand and the work are understood, then raise it; take easy, well-paid work whenever it comes, to make the profit
as large as possible. Each week Claude reviews itself, writes the lessons in `LESSONS.md` and improves the next week.
Spending money stays the owner's call.

## Targets

- **Step 2: break-even on everything, USD 300+ a month net of platform fees.**
- Then a monthly profit that keeps coming in, raised as the brand grows.

## The decision

We sell what we have already proven we can do: Roblox development, and turning 3D models into models ready for
Roblox. Services come first: they bring cash and reviews, and they show what people pay for. Products come second,
made from the reusable parts of what clients order most.

Rejected:

- Trading and crypto: we have no edge, and it puts the capital at risk.
- AI content farms: search engines and video platforms demote them, and they take 6–12 months to earn.
- Dropshipping and print-on-demand: they need ad money and customer service, and the margins are thin.
- "Click to earn" schemes and airdrops.
- Cold email: Australia's Spam Act 2003.

The evidence (2026-10-25): generic pet systems on BuiltByBit sell for USD 9–16 and show 0 purchases. A generic kit
does not sell by itself, so a product must come from demand we have already seen.

Roblox scripting gigs on Fiverr range from about USD 50 to 1,400 a package (a sample of live gigs, 2026-10-25). Ours
start in the low middle (45 / 120 / 280) to win the first reviews; prices go up after 5 reviews.

## Phases and gates

1. **Weeks 1–4: listings live.**
   - Fiverr G1 and G2 and the Upwork Agency are online, with up to 2 strong-fit proposals a day on Upwork, within the Connects
     budget (`listings/upwork-log.md`).
   - Gate: 3 orders delivered with 5-star reviews.
   - If there is no order after 3 weeks: change one thing a week (title, picture, price, a cheaper entry package).
2. **Weeks 5–12: grow.**
   - Prices go up after 5 reviews; we ask for repeat work.
   - Gate: USD 300 net in one month.
3. **Month 2 and after: product #1.**
   - Built from the reusable part of the gig that sells most.
   - Gate: 10 sales without ads before any money goes into ads.
4. **Then:** one product a month, and S4 if the owner wants it.

## The weekly round (every Monday morning by the scheduled task `weekly-business-review`; or when the owner says "tuần kinh doanh")

1. Read the numbers of each platform in the desktop app's built-in browser, where the owner is signed in:
   - Fiverr: impressions, clicks, orders and messages for each gig;
   - Upwork: proposals, views, interviews, contracts, Connects left, earnings.
2. Fill the KPI row of the week. At the start of a month, fill the ledger row (`LEDGER.md`).
3. Review the week in `LESSONS.md`: what worked, what did not and why, profit against spending, and the lessons.
4. Make one change to the listings, so that each number moves for one known reason; adjust the proposal rules in
   `listings/upwork-log.md` if the lessons say so; set the work queue.

## Rules

- **Honest.**
  - Every listing says the work is AI-assisted (Fiverr asks for transparency).
  - Every delivery is tested in Roblox Studio before it goes out.
  - No fake reviews or review swaps; a refund when we fail.
- **Nothing copied.**
  - No code, models or names from other games.
  - Nothing from a store is resold.
  - Egg Heist is not sold as a kit, because that would make clones of the owner's own game.
  - Products are written fresh.
- **Nothing private in this repository.**
  - No passwords or tokens.
  - Clients' files stay outside it (`orders/` is ignored by git).
