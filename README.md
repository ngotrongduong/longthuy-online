# Long Thuy — online income

Long Thuy Pty Ltd sells Roblox development and 3D models made ready for Roblox: services on Fiverr and Upwork first,
then digital products. The work is done by Claude; the owner does what needs his identity.

- `PLAN.md`: the plan, its phases and gates, the KPI table.
- `NEXT_STEPS.md`: the work queue.
- `LEDGER.md`: the money, month by month.
- `listings/`: the Fiverr gigs and the Upwork profile, ready to paste.
- `CLAUDE.md`: how Claude works here.

- Bước tiếp theo nằm trong `NEXT_STEPS.md`.
