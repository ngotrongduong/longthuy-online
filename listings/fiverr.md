**Packages**

| Test place | — | yes | yes |

**Description** (1,200 max)

Need a system in your Roblox game that just works? I build server-authoritative Luau systems that hold up against
exploiters, tested in Roblox Studio before delivery.

What I build:
- Robux shops: game passes and developer products with a receipt handler that never loses or doubles a purchase
- Data saving: DataStore with session locking, retries and safe defaults
- Pets and eggs: hatching with the odds shown to players, pets that follow, inventory
- NPCs and bots: walking, idling, simple AI
- Leaderboards, daily rewards, codes, quests
- Bug fixes and reviews of existing scripts

How it works: you describe what you need, I confirm the scope and price, you get a ready-to-insert model (.rbxm) or a
Rojo project, a test place and a README. Clean, commented code that you own.

Transparency: I use AI-assisted tools and my own test harness; every line is reviewed and tested in Studio before you
get it.

Not sure which package fits? Message me first.

**FAQ**

  needs changes first, I say so before you order.
- *Do I own the code?* Yes, for use in your own games.
- *Do you use free models or store scripts?* No.
- *Do you make UI?* I connect your UI to the systems and can make simple UI; models are in my other gig.
- *Rojo or Studio?* Either.

**Requirements (asked at order)**: the system and how it should behave; your place file or the scripts involved (or
"new game"); the deadline; games you like, for the feel only.

**Tags (5)**: roblox, roblox scripting, luau, roblox developer, roblox game

## G2 — 3D model ready for Roblox

**Title**: I will make your 3D model Roblox ready, low poly and rigged

**Category**: Graphics & Design > 3D Design > 3D Game Models (or Programming & Tech > Game Development > Roblox)

**Packages**

| | Basic — Optimise | Standard — Optimise + rig | Premium — Rig + animation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | USD 30 | USD 75 | USD 150 |
| Delivery | 2 days | 4 days | 7 days |
| Revisions | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Scope | One static model: fewer triangles, texture baked and resized, scale, ground, pivot; imported as a MeshPart | Plus a skeleton and skin, checked joint by joint for clean bending | Plus a walk cycle and an idle as Roblox animations, in a test place |

**Description**

Got a 3D model from Meshy, Tripo, Blender or anywhere else that looks wrong in Roblox: too many triangles, broken
textures, wrong size, no skeleton? I turn it into a model that is ready for Roblox.

What you get:
- Low-poly optimisation under Roblox's limits, lighter for phones
- Texture baked and resized; the model scaled, standing on the ground, pivot set
- A skeleton and skin for creatures and characters (four-legged animals, birds, monsters), each joint checked for
  clean bending
- A walk and an idle animation (Premium)
- Delivered as .rbxm plus .fbx/.glb, with a test place

Transparency: my own Blender pipeline with AI-assisted tools; every model is checked in Roblox Studio before
delivery. You must own the rights to the model; I do not take models ripped from other games.

Send the file and a picture of how it should look before ordering if you are unsure.

**FAQ**
- *Which files?* .glb, .gltf, .fbx, .obj.
- *How light?* Your budget if you have one; otherwise as light as the look allows.
- *Do you model from scratch?* Not in this gig.

**Requirements**: the model file; a picture of the look; the size in studs; a triangle budget if any; which
animations (Premium).
