## Description

Six stylized low-poly forest animals for your game: **fox, wolf, deer, bear, boar and rabbit**. Each one is a
single mesh with one small palette texture, rigged with a clean quadruped skeleton and animated with four
seamless loops.

**In the pack**
- 6 animals, 3,100 to 4,100 triangles each, one material and one 256 px texture each (flat colours, easy to recolour)
- 4 looping animations per animal (24 in all), 30 fps, in place: **Idle**, **Walk**, **Run** (the rabbit hops), **Eat**
- Move speeds given for every Walk and Run, so the feet do not slide
- Consistent bone names across the pack (Root, Hips, Spine, Neck, Head, 4 bones per leg), max 4 influences per vertex
- Files: FBX (all clips), one FBX per clip (for Roblox's Animation Editor), GLB/glTF, .blend source, PNG textures

**Roblox ready**
- Under Roblox's MeshPart limit, real-world scale, pivot at the feet
- Step-by-step import guide: Import 3D as a custom rig, then each clip through the Animation Editor

**Also works in** Unity (Generic rig), Godot 4 (GLB) and Unreal (FBX). Import notes for each are
in the README.

**Not included:** turn, jump, attack, death or sleep clips, root motion, sounds, AI scripts, LODs. The wolf's walk
is a trot. The motion is procedural (keyed curves and a leg solver), not motion capture.

**Licence:** use in commercial and non-commercial games and projects; do not resell or redistribute the models
themselves, and do not use them to train AI. Made by Long Thuy (Australia), built with our own procedural Blender tools.
