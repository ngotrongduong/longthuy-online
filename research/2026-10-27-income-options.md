# Other ways to earn (research of 2026-10-27)

Scored by: fit with what we can already do (Roblox Luau systems, procedural low-poly models with rigs and
animation in Blender, tested in Studio), money needed (we spend nothing up front), time to the first dollar, and
what only the owner can do (identity, payouts).

## Ranked

| # | Way | How money comes | Why it fits | Owner must | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Roblox Creator Store, paid models and plugins (USD)** | Models USD 2.99–49.99, plugins 4.99–249.99; we keep 100 % minus tax and card fees; 30-day escrow, paid monthly via Stripe | Sells to Roblox developers, the buyers we already target; reuses our systems and creature pipeline | ID-verified Roblox account with 2-step verification, Stripe seller account (bank details) | **Start now** |
| 2 | **Rigged, animated low-poly animal pack** sold on the Creator Store (Roblox version) and on Fab (88 %), itch.io, Unity Asset Store | One-off sales, USD 5–30 a pack | Our fox pipeline makes more animals by script; animated skinned creatures ready for Roblox are scarce | Seller accounts and payout details on each store | **Start after product 1** |
| 3 | **Roblox Talent Hub, DevForum and hiring Discords** | Commissions and part-time scripter roles (posts offer USD 1,600–2,400 a month, or Robux) | No platform fee; Roblox-only demand; our live games are the proof | Uses his Roblox account; payment is off-platform (PayPal), so ask for milestones up front | **Start now** (scam filter as on Fiverr) |
| 4 | **Contra** (0 % commission freelance network) | Same services as Upwork, no fee to us | One more storefront for the same portfolio | Sign up, payout details | Later, cheap |
| 5 | **Paid open-source bounties (Algora)** | USD per merged pull request, via Stripe, fee about 9–11 % | Pure coding, no client chat | GitHub account; check Australia is supported | Try one when idle; many projects refuse AI-written PRs |
| 6 | Roblox UGC avatar items | 30 % of each sale in Robux, 600 Robux advance per item, cash only via DevEx from 30,000 Robux | We can model accessories | Upload fees in Robux | **No for now**: slow, small, needs money up front |
| 7 | Local websites with a care plan (S4 in PLAN) | AUD 49–99 a month each | We can build sites | Selling face to face | When the owner wants it |

Rejected again: data-labelling and "AI trainer" sites (they need the person himself and forbid AI help), content
farms, trading and crypto, dropshipping, cold email (Spam Act 2003).
