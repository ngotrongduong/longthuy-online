# Free credits: Meshy and Tripo

The daily scheduled task `free-credits-daily` claims what it can and adds one line per run here.

## Where the free credits are (checked 2026-11-15)

Meshy (meshy.ai/settings/subscription, "Get Free Credits"):
- Done once: Connect with Discord 100/100, Follow Popular Creators 100/100, Connect Social Accounts 50/50 (GitHub
  link on the profile).
- Daily: Share on X, up to 40 a day. The X share button is in a generation's share panel; Meshy pays 10 credits for
  each click other people make on the link (one share counts once per generation). The owner's X account is signed in
  in his Chrome; at most one post a day, only about our own models.
- Repeatable: Publish to Community, 50 for each piece Meshy features (only models we will not sell).
- Owner only: Write G2 Review, 500 once (his G2 sign-in and his own honest review). Refer a Friend: 500 when a
  friend subscribes.
- Contributor Program (Discord, Myko): 1,000 credits or a Pro month on acceptance; needs the owner's Discord.

Tripo (studio.tripo3d.ai/creator-growth#rewards):
- Weekly: Submit to "Feature My Model" +20 credits, and enable "Submit Simultaneously" +20 credits.
- Long term: each featured model +20 credits. Discord link +50 already obtained.
- Daily tasks give only EXP (levels: L3 gives USD 2 Tripo Cash a month, L4 USD 5; Tripo Cash is money off a
  purchase, not credits). The owner wants the level anyway (2026-11-15). EXP we can earn ourselves: Generate a Model
  +10 a day (30 credits); one-time tasks all done 11-15. EXP from others (downloads +10, recrafts +10, collects +5,
  likes +1, followers +5) comes only from real people. Subscription renewal gives +100. Lv2 = 500 EXP.
- Emails: surveys sometimes pay 300 credits (the owner fills them in himself).
- Referral (studio.tripo3d.ai/referral, code POI5PR): 30 credits for each qualified share (daily and yearly
  limits); 300 credits to both sides when a new user signs up through the link; 1,000 credits plus reward balance when
  that user pays within 30 days. Cap 6,500 credits and USD 120 a year. Share button "Twitter" opens an X post
  prefilled by Tripo. Posting on X from the owner's account: Claude's tools refuse to press Post by themselves
  (2026-11-15), so the post is left open and the owner presses Post, unless he gives a standing yes that the tool accepts.
